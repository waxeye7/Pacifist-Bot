## What actually moved the needle

**1. 6 upgraders after 550 (cycle-4).**  
RCL2 is 45k upgrade. After slam-5 the cap is 550 and `[2W,2C,2M]` shuttles deliver ~4 e/t. Four of them leave energy on the floor. Six cost spawn time early (we lost RCL2 by 56) and pay it back on the 135k climb. **Keep.** Do not run 6 during the 5-ext slam.

**2. Leftover-5 actually holding (cycle-5).**  
RCL3 unlocks 10 extensions. The next five cost 15k and do not buy a bigger 4W (500e already fits at 550). Cycle-2 tried this and **leaked**: race rooms have no `planV2`, so `placeFromBasePlan` + checkerboard sited all 10. Cycle-5 closed that — both paths call `extensionTake` (5 through RCL3, engine cap at RCL4 after storage). Live check: candidate L3 rooms sat at 5 ext, control at 10. RCL3 −1156. RCL4 7/8 because E5S3 missed the 40k budget; the seven finishers were ~4.7k faster than cycle-4. **Keep.** Those five still get built at RCL4 — delay, not skip.

**Tried and sent back:** HOL+recycle as a bundle (cycle-1 lost RCL3). Instant leftover-5. 6W miner (same 10 e/t). Trickle-ext. RCL1 HOL (cycle-6, RCL4 +1321). Recycle 200e at 550 (cycle-7: suicide walks the 1W off the controller; RCL3 +1466).

**Now racing:** cycle-8 sites a controller depot on the legacy path. Race rooms have no planV2, so the 4W never parked unless a leftover box happened to sit near the controller. Clean wipe this seed.

Control stays `e839fc8`. We do not `push-race` on a 7/8 RCL4.

## Expansion — not "next room found" (live is a different list)

**Online** AutoExpand does not look around. It takes the first still-unowned name in segment 86.

**Local pack** (`push-expansion-pack.mjs`): plan quality − 2.5×distance, dist 2–5, top 20.

**Live pack** (`push-live-expansion.mjs`) — this is what claimed E36N57:

```
score = 100 − 20·dist − 40·swampFrac + 5·min(spawnOpen,6) + (mineral≠H ? 15 : 0)
```

Dist 1–3, 2 sources, not highway/SK. **No plan** is pushed. Distance dominates: a weak neighbour beats a good room two steps further.

Also not pack-ranked: if `CanClaimRemote >= 3`, a remote can be **claimed as an owned room** (`rooms.spawning.ts`). Observer WallClearer can claim-then-unclaim leftover RCL0 rooms. No vision ⇒ pack treats a room as free.

Do not unclaim E36N57. Re-push the live pack after it so the next GCL is re-ranked. `Memory.features.autoExpand = false` until then if you want a hand pick via `SC()`.

## Live E39N58 — spawn already there

Three stacked rules:

1. RCL1–6 spawn cap is **1**. Any of *our* spawns fills it.
2. `migrateSpawns` **kept** an off-plan only-spawn until RCL7.
3. `ensureSpawnSite` swallowed `ERR_INVALID_TARGET` when a structure sat on the planned tile.

So a leftover or colonise spawn (wrong tile) meant the plan spawn never sited, and you could not place another.

**Fix in src (not pushed over the local race):** young room (RCL≤3, no storage) retires an idle off-plan only-spawn; spawn-first / AutoExpand clear the plan tile before siting; blocked tiles are logged.

If the leftover spawn is **someone else's**, we cannot destroy it — the log will say `foreign spawn`. Then the tile has to be attacked, or we pick a neighbour (not done yet).

## VPS / live energy bleed

RCL4+ with storage used 8 open sites. Roads + personal ramparts + migrate kept them full. Builders drained the bank; fillers/upgraders starved; the room felt dead. Off-plan ramparts from the pre-rebuild planner are a separate migrate job (`migratePlan` on established rooms — adoption does not auto-demolish a grown base).

**Fix in src:**
- RCL6+ bank below 30k/80k/150k → no new sites; strip road/rampart *sites*
- Roads-only at RCL6+ does not keep a builder when broke
- Builder withdraw floor matches that stockpile (was 300)
- Rampart sites count toward the 8-slot budget at RCL6+
- Repair / miners only nurse **plan** ramparts — old stamp walls can decay
- RCL8 “+5 builders if cap < 2000” only if bank ≥ 50k

RCL4–5 dump unchanged. Leftover-5 unchanged.

E37N59 rebuild is the intended path: plan adopted, gradual migrate. That is working as designed.
